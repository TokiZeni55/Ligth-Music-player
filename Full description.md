# Ligth Player

Ligth Player is a lightweight and privacy focused music player for Windows. It is made for people who want to listen to their local music without online features, background services or high system demands.

The main goal of Ligth Player is to give an comfortable music listening experience. It uses few system resources as possible. It is built for use. It is designed to stay out of the way while you work, study browse the web, play games or do tasks on your computer.

Ligth Player supports playing your local MP3 and WAV files directly from your computer. You can organize your music using playlists. You can mark songs as favorites. You can search through your music collection. You can control playback with shuffle and repeat modes.

It also includes themes so the player can be customized to match your preferred look. The interface stays simple and easy to use. It does not fill the screen with controls.

One of the focuses of Ligth Player is resource efficiency. Under usage it can use as little as 40 MB of RAM around 0.2 percent CPU usage and around 0.1 percent GPU usage. Actual resource usage can vary depending on the system the file being played, the window state and other background applications.

Ligth Player works completely with music files. It does not require an account or cloud storage. Your music stays on your computer. This makes it suitable for users who prefer a local and private music library.

## Performance

Ligth Player is designed to remain extremely lightweight while running in the background or alongside other applications.

Typical resource usage can be as low, as:

RAM: 40 MB

CPU: 0.2 percent

GPU: 0.1 percent

These numbers are intended as examples of the resource usage the player is designed to achieve. They may vary depending on the computer and workload.

Ligth Player is made for people who want a music player that plays their files. It does not turn music playback into a resource application.

## Technology

Ligth Player is built with Python and CustomTkinter. Local audio playback is handled through Windows system functionality.

The project focuses on simplicity, low resource usage, offline functionality and a clean user experience.
